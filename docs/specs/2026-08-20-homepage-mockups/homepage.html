One thing the price forced into the open

Writing this block made me check what $29 actually buys, and the answer matters for the copy: the audit is free — three findings, then an email to see the rest. The $29 is monitoring: re-audits, diffs, up to SITE_LIMIT = 3 sites and AUDITS_PER_MONTH = 10 re-audits a month. That's why it's two rows rather than one number. A lone "$29" above the button would have told visitors it costs $29 to try, which isn't true and would have been the only false claim on the page.
