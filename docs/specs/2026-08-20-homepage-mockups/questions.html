Three decisions baked into what you just clicked

1

The URL is step 1, not a separate screen

Six steps total. It's the only required field, so it gets the only step without a Skip button and the only progress note that says it's needed.

2

"Skip" is a real button, not small print

Every answer is optional in profile.ts and the profiler is built to come back honest about gaps. A stepper that hides the exit would quietly pressure people into filling boxes with noise — and noise in these answers is worse than a blank, because the spawn rules read them.

3

Question 2 tells you why it matters

"It decides which reviewers we put on the page" is true — concerns[0] is what breaks the tie when more spawn rules fire than the cap of 4 allows. Telling people that gets better answers than a blank box does.
